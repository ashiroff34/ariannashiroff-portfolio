03

Product · Beta · 100-Person Waitlist

Thyself

An app for personality typology. Enneagram-centered, but built to hold the full stack: MBTI, Socionics, Instincts, Attitudinals. 04

Nonprofit · Co-Founder · Vice President

Pangea Kids

A nonprofit I co-founded at 15, where I served as Vice President. Supplies to clinics in Saigon. Fifteen chapters. $10K raised. Two thousand items donated. But the thing I actually learned: the hard part of medicine in low-resource settings isn't clinical. It's the translation layer — the gap between what donors think they're giving and what a nurse actually needs at 6 a.m. That gap is the whole problem. It's worth closing.

05

Policy Brief · ENGL 138T · Penn State

Good Intentions Are Not Governance

Written for ENGL 138T and addressed to Pfizer's CMO and CSO. Full title: "Good Intentions Are Not Governance: The Case Against Pfizer's Current AI Framework for Clinical Trial Patient Selection." The argument: there's a five-year gap between what Pfizer can do with AI in clinical trial patient selection and what it has any accountability structure for. Four binding recommendations to close it. This is the intersection I keep ending up in — where the clinical meets the algorithmic, where capability has outrun accountability, and someone has to ask what governance actually looks like.

08

Essay · Writing

The Intuition Beneath Networking

An essay on what genuine connection actually runs on, because social intuition is not the same as social strategy. It draws on William James and contemporary research in social cognition to make the argument that the most consequential relationships in your life probably weren't planned. They happened because you let something open in you and followed it. The transactional framework for networking misses the entire point.
