03

Nonprofit · Co-Founder · Vice President

Pangea Kids

A nonprofit getting medical supplies to clinics in Saigon. I co-founded it at 15 and was Vice President. Fifteen chapters across schools. $10K raised. Around two thousand items donated. Most of the actual work was logistics: figuring out which clinic needed what, when, and how to get it there in usable condition. That part of low-resource medicine is way harder than the clinical part.

04

Policy Brief · ENGL 138T · Penn State

Good Intentions Are Not Governance

A policy brief written for ENGL 138T and addressed to Pfizer's CMO and CSO. Full title: "Good Intentions Are Not Governance: The Case Against Pfizer's Current AI Framework for Clinical Trial Patient Selection." The argument is that Pfizer's AI capability for selecting patients in clinical trials has outpaced its accountability structure by roughly five years. The brief lays out four binding recommendations for closing that gap.
