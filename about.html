About

The project is translation.

I co-founded VMA at 15 and ended up Vice President before I knew how to run a meeting. We were getting supplies to clinics in Saigon and I figured something out fast: the bottleneck in under-resourced medicine isn't clinical. It's the translation layer. The gap between what donors think they're giving and what a nurse actually needs at 6 a.m. — that's not a small gap. That gap is the whole problem.

That same gap is in everything I've done since. Pfizer. Saint Luke's. A Penn State lab where I edited E. coli with CRISPR-Cas9. Ten million people on @thescispot, because research that only lives in journals never reaches the people it was written for. Thyself, because interior systems deserve the same rigor we give clinical ones — and that's not something we treat as obvious. The surface keeps changing. The project has always been translation.
