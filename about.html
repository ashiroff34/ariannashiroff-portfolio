About

The project is translation.

I've always been more interested in why systems work than what they produce, and I've learned that's a harder thing to care about. I co-founded VMA, the Vietnam Medical Aid Foundation, at 15 and ended up Vice President before I knew how to run a meeting. We were getting supplies to clinics in Saigon and figuring something out in real time: the bottleneck in under-resourced medicine isn't clinical. It's the translation layer. The gap between what donors think they're giving and what a nurse actually needs at 6 a.m. is not a small gap.

That gap shows up in everything I do. Pfizer. Saint Luke's. A Penn State lab where I edited E. coli with CRISPR-Cas9. Ten million people on @thescispot, because research that lives only in journals is research that never reaches the people it was written for. Thyself, because interior systems deserve the same rigor we give clinical ones. It's both the same work and a completely different surface every time. The project has always been translation.
